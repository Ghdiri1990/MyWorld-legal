Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

1. Who we are

My World ("the app", "we") is a social media app operated by New Horizon. You can contact us at Ghdiri1990@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account data: your email address and password (the password is stored in hashed form by our authentication provider).
  • Profile data: your name, nickname, bio, profile photo, and your birth date together with the visibility setting you choose for it.
  • Content you create: posts (photos or videos, descriptions, categories, languages, countries), comments, likes and saved posts.
  • Social data: who you follow, who follows you, and your block, restrict and VIP lists.
  • Messages you send to other users (see section 5).
  • Wallet data: your Riyom balance and the history of Riyom you received or spent.
  • Reports and support messages you send to us.
  • Preferences you set in the app.
  • Technical data that our service providers keep to run the service (for example IP addresses in server logs).

We do not show ads, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

3. How we use your information

To create and run your account, show your content to the people you choose, deliver messages, calculate your Riyom balance, keep the service safe (including reviewing reports), and improve the app.

4. What other people can see

Your public profile and your public posts are visible to other users according to the privacy setting of each post. Your comments are visible on the posts you comment on. Your messages are visible only to the person you send them to.

5. Messages disappear after 48 hours

Private messages are automatically and permanently deleted 48 hours after they are sent. Deletion runs automatically every few minutes, and messages older than 48 hours are hidden immediately.

6. Riyom

Riyom is a virtual in-app point. It has no cash value and cannot be bought, sold or withdrawn. Earned Riyom expires 3 months after the month in which it was earned.

7. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We use these service providers to run the app:

  • Supabase - database and authentication.
  • Cloudflare - storage and delivery of photos and videos.

These providers may process data in countries other than yours. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect people's safety.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account, profile and content: until you delete your account.
  • Messages: 48 hours.
  • Earned Riyom: 3 months from the month in which it was earned.
  • Backups kept by our providers may hold residual copies for a limited period after deletion.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Take a break: Settings, then Leave, then "Take a break" hides your account and posts until you sign in again.
  • Delete your account: Settings, then Leave, then "Delete account". This permanently deletes your profile, posts, comments, likes, follows, messages and Riyom balance.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data by emailing Ghdiri1990@gmail.com.

10. Age requirement

My World is intended only for people who are 18 years old or older. If you believe a person under 18 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use industry-standard protections (encrypted connections and access controls). No online service can be 100% secure.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the app.

13. Contact

New Horizon - Ghdiri1990@gmail.com
